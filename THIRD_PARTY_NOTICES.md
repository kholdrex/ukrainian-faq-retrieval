# Third-party data

`data/queries_test.jsonl`, `data/queries_dev.jsonl`, `data/pools.json`, `results/expansions.jsonl` and
`results/per_query.jsonl` contain question ids and question texts from WebFAQ retrieval
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/PaDaS-Lab/webfaq-retrieval, configurations `ukr-corpus`, `ukr-queries`,
`ukr-qrels`), published by PaDaS-Lab under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The answer collection is not stored; `uafaq data` rebuilds it.

The MIT licence of this repository covers the code.
